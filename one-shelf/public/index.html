      05Questions

      Good questions

      Everything on the shelf is roasted inside Greater London. Enter your postcode and each bag shows roughly how far its roastery is from you. Distances are straight-line miles from the middle of your postcode district, so they are approximate.

      Each bag is listed with the name of the roastery that roasted it, and you can filter the shelf to a single roaster.

      Yes, when everything in your basket comes from one roastery that offers collection. Choose collection in the basket and pick it up from the roastery counter. Baskets that mix roasteries are sent by local delivery.

      Open a coffee to choose a grind for how you brew: espresso, moka pot, filter or cafetière. If you own a grinder, whole bean will always taste better for longer.

      After you pay you land on your order page. Keep that link: it shows the current status of your order, and where to collect it or where it is being delivered.
